## Material corrections

- #403 is a guarded barrel interaction, not an unlisted warning row; #407 is the tavern-cellar handoff, not pumpkin gathering.
- #412 drops are from leper gnomes outside Gnomeregan; the prior full-group dungeon warning was incorrect.
- #417 is not verified as an elite group quest; #422’s trigger is a Moonrage Darkrunner at the remedy, not Son of Arugal. #423–424 now describe the actual worgen camps and Deep Elem Mine objectives.
- #434 remains HIGH for the paired ambush and short guard window, without claiming a mandatory full group. #435 has no verified scripted waves.
- #446, #439–442, #444, and #492 are delivery or handoff steps with no required combat.
- #452 was reduced from EXTREME to HIGH because exact elite wave counts and wipe reports were not established by the available Classic text. The quest requests help against the Council; the full event mechanics remain uncertain.
- #474 no longer calls Nek’rosh an elite; available Classic comments describe mixed near-level solo outcomes in a crowded camp.
- #485 is the beacon handoff; the robot escort is a separate follow-up. #498 is a guarded prisoner rescue, not a scripted escort-wave quest.
