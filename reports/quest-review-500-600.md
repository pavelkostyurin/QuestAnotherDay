## Material corrections

- #502 turns Stanley hostile after the elixir interaction, with nearby farm mobs able to join. #506, #508, #510–511, #513–514, #521–525, #538, #542–543, #550–551, #554, #558, #560–561, #563, #579, #583, #588, #592, #597, and #599 are handoffs, dialogue, or item-placement steps; their own objectives do not justify the previous generic combat warnings.
- #504 and #519 involve the Crushridge ogre ruins; visible Classic-page comments support dangerous elite/patrol fights but do not establish a mandatory full group. #520 is the Mug’Thol boss encounter, rated HIGH with area danger kept explicitly inferential.
- #532 takes the player into Hillsbrad Town for combat and theft objectives. #541 is the final Dun Garok siege with a broad kill list. #553 returns the Horde player to heavily guarded Southshore for the grave interaction.
- #544 has a multi-building Dalaran camp route with guards and casters in the sampled comments; solo reports support HIGH, not a mandatory-group claim. #553 requires a grave interaction in heavily guarded Southshore; the previous claims about universal one-shots and unavoidable deaths are not supported. #567’s text verifies four separate bounty targets but does not support the old blanket “mobs level 35–40” warning.
- Sparse descriptions for #516–517, #526, #530–531, #533, #535, #537, #540, #552, #556–557, #565, #570–572, #579–580, #584–585, #589, and #591–595 remain lower-confidence where location or combat mechanics are not specified. No exact levels, respawn times, or drop rates are asserted.
