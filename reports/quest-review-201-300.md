## Evidence and unresolved catalog note

- #202: Classic databases classify Colonel Kurzen as a group quest and place his officers in the Stockpile cave. [ClassicDB](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=202), [Classic WoW database](https://datenbank.classic-wow.de/?quest=202).
- #206 and #208: retrieved Classic data identifies Mai’Zoth and King Bangalash as high-level Elite targets; Bangalash is a group quest and Mai’Zoth has elite guards. [Mai’Zoth](https://classic.warcraftdb.com/data/quest/206), [Big Game Hunter](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=208).
- #214: bandanas can be collected outside Deadmines, but the outside sources are Elite Defias. [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-classic/deadmines-quests-guide), [Zockify](https://www.zockify.com/classic/dungeons/deadmines/).
- #219: Keeshan’s escort is through the Blackrock Orc camp in Redridge; groups and extra pulls are the material danger. [ClassicDB](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=219).
- #235: the Classic quest page identifies “The Ashenvale Hunt” as Horde and describes its first step as a dialogue hand-in at Senani; the pinned catalog currently labels it Alliance. I updated the warning for the dialogue-only step but left catalog faction metadata untouched. [Wowhead Classic](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=235/the-ashenvale-hunt).
- #248 and #253: both are Elite quests; Stonewatch Tower has Elite guards, and Eliza is a level-31 Elite who summons guardians. [Looking Further](https://theforeverera.com/en/quests/248/), [Bride of the Embalmer](https://wowclassicdb.com/quest/253), [ClassicDB comments](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=253).
- #272: the aquatic-form trial requires deep dives around hostile threshers and an Elite reef shark; the Westfall bubbling fissure provides a safe breath reset. [Wowhead Classic](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=272/trial-of-the-sea-lion).
- #278–280: the reagent stage (#278) is group-flagged because it sends low-level characters after Elite ogres; the later dam interaction (#280) is hazardous chiefly because it is underwater. [ClassicDB #278](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=278), [Wowhead Classic #280](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=280/a-dark-threat-looms).
- #289–290: shipwreck fighting and the submerged strongbox create crowded underwater pulls and breath pressure; solo approaches exist, so #290 is HIGH rather than EXTREME. [ClassicDB #289](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=289), [Wowhead Classic #290](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=290/lifting-the-curse).
