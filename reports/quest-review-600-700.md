## Material corrections

- #602, #623, #635–638, #641, #646, #649–650, #653, #655, #657, #659, #661, #663, #668–670, #674–675, #683, #686–688, #690, #695, #697, and #700 are dialogue, exploration, or delivery steps. Their objectives do not justify direct combat warnings; route hazards remain noted only where relevant. #619 is an interaction that sets up the Negolash encounter, so it is rated HIGH and warns players to prepare before triggering it.
- #604’s Classic comments show dense camps and a solo challenge, but not an unavoidable group requirement; reduced from EXTREME to MEDIUM. #608 remains HIGH because the three targets occupy close-quarters ships and the quest asks for skilled comrades. #618 remains EXTREME as a level 52 elite encounter that is not a reasonable solo attempt near the quest level.
- #648 and #660 are genuine escorts; #665 is an escort/defense event near a cave ambush. #667’s cannon and crew make the attack event substantially safer than its previous generic underwater warning, so it is LOW.
- #656 is HIGH rather than a blanket group requirement: Classic reports show repeated elemental adds and class-dependent solo attempts above the intended level. #685 remains HIGH because both bounty targets are together in guarded Stromgarde Keep and the quest text calls for skilled comrades.
- Many prior reasons included exact levels, respawn claims, or unrelated creature types without traceable support. Revised ledger reasons remove those specifics; model-knowledge reviews use medium confidence at most, with low confidence retained where local context remains sparse.
