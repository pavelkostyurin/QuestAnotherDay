# Quest Another Day

Quest Another Day helps you make safer questing decisions in **World of Warcraft Classic Era Hardcore** by highlighting quests that pose a risk to solo players.

Reviewed quests display a clear threat rating alongside a short explanation of the danger. Hover over a threat icon to read the warning before attempting the quest.

## Threat ratings

- **Low — Green circle:** No unusual solo hazard identified.
- **Medium — Yellow circle:** Positioning, extra enemies, or other meaningful risks require care.
- **High — Orange circle:** Hidden or unusually severe solo hazards.
- **Extreme — Red ×:** Not recommended for a solo Hardcore character.

Threat icons appear beside quest names in the **Questie tracker**, or in **Blizzard's standard quest log** when Questie is not loaded. Questie is supported but not required.

The addon also prints guidance in chat when you accept a reviewed quest. Acceptance warnings are enabled by default and can be toggled with `/qad on` and `/qad off`.

## Settings and commands

Open **Options → AddOns → Quest Another Day**, or enter `/qad options`.

The optional **Display raid / dungeon quests** setting enables threat icons and acceptance warnings for reviewed dungeon and raid quests. It is off by default to keep the addon focused on solo questing.

- `/qad` — List reviewed quests currently in your quest log.
- `/qad <quest ID>` — Look up a quest's rating and warning.
- `/qad status` — Check acceptance warnings, quest icons, and dungeon / raid display status.

## Feedback and support

Found incorrect guidance or have a suggestion? Open `/qad options` to report it on GitHub or contact the author on Discord. Quest reports provide copyable details for the last threat tooltip you hovered, so check that the quest matches your report.

Ratings assume solo play near the quest's intended level with ordinary gear. They are guidance, never a guarantee of safety. A missing rating does not mean a quest is safe.

Quest Another Day is currently available in English.
