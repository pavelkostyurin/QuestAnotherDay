# Quest Another Day v0.44.400

- Expanded and corrected quest guidance across Classic Era, with reviews through quest ID 400. Fixed quest identities and chain stages, and replaced warnings that described earlier steps or misstated locations.
- Updated ratings and advice for specific hazards: operating Samophlange valves can summon enemies, Defias Pillagers can deal heavy spell damage, and underwater searches can put you at risk of drowning.
- Quest tooltips now show assessment confidence. Low-confidence warnings also explain that evidence is limited and mechanics may vary.
- Questie threat icons now sit more closely in line with quest names.
