# Quest Another Day v0.43.3

- **Expanded quest guidance:** Added ratings and warnings for 468 quests, including Silithus and Ahn’Qiraj content, Naxxramas, holiday quests, and event turn-ins. New guidance highlights elite encounters, dangerous travel, and accidental PvP flagging during Field Duty.
- **Corrected threat rating:** Slaying the Beast is now rated Extreme, matching its warning that Taragaman requires a full dungeon group.
- **Clearer, smaller icons:** Replaced raid markers with outlined 10-pixel icons: green, yellow, and orange circles for Low, Medium, and High, and a red × for Extreme. Questie and Blizzard quest-log icons retain larger hover and click targets.
- **Reduced quest-data memory use:** The addon now retains shared warnings and your faction’s quest guidance, skipping opposite-faction records. Direct lookups for opposite-faction quests return Unknown.
