# Quest Another Day v0.45.1

- Reworked quest guidance across Classic Era with clearer, quest-specific hazards and advice. For example, the Crown of Will warnings distinguish its dangerous elite-target steps from the final hand-in, which adds no combat.
- All 678 quests flagged as dungeon or raid quests now carry an **Extreme** rating and advise bringing a suitable group or skipping them on Hardcore. Dungeon and raid display remains off by default.
- Warnings that reference another quest now show its quoted name in that quest’s threat color, without the quest ID. `/qad list` and quest ID lookups also color quest titles by threat.
- Tooltips now focus on the warning and threat rating; confidence labels and the limited-evidence note have been removed.
